# Skill images

All images are stored locally; no third-party image requests are made by visitors.

- Technology logos, including LÖVE2D: [Devicon](https://github.com/devicons/devicon), MIT (see devicon-LICENSE.txt).
- Obsidian: [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), CC0. Color adjusted for contrast.
- FlutterFlow: [official website favicon](https://www.flutterflow.io/favicon-32.png). Its white background is visually removed with `mix-blend-mode: multiply` on the badge. Product mark belongs to FlutterFlow.
- AI, neural networks, data analysis, automation and XML: [Lucide](https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide), ISC / MIT (see lucide-LICENSE.txt). Stroke color adjusted for contrast.

Product names and marks belong to their respective owners.
